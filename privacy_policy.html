🔒 Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us

Last Updated: December 19, 2025

Privacy at a Glance

Receipt prioritizes your privacy. We don't use ads, trackers, or analytics. All your receipts are stored locally on your device – we never see your financial data.

ℹ️ Information We Collect

Receipt collects minimal information only when you actively use specific features:

Data Type Purpose When Collected
Receipt Images Store and display your scanned receipts When you scan or import receipts
Receipt Data Store extracted text and purchase details When you scan receipts
App Preferences Remember your settings and preferences When you customize the app
Important: All data processing happens locally on your device. Receipt does not collect any background data, analytics, or personal information.

💾 Local Data Storage

Receipt stores all your data locally on your device. This includes:

  • Scanned receipt images
  • Extracted text and purchase information
  • Categories and tags you create
  • Your app preferences and settings

📷 Camera Usage

Receipt uses your device's camera to scan receipts:

  • Camera access is only activated when you choose to scan
  • Photos are processed and stored locally on your device
  • No images are ever uploaded to external servers
  • You can revoke camera access at any time in iOS Settings

🔗 Third-Party Services

Receipt is designed to work completely offline. We do not use any third-party services for:

  • Data storage or cloud sync
  • Analytics or tracking
  • Advertising
  • User profiling
100% Offline: Receipt works entirely on your device without requiring an internet connection.

🔒 Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your personal information or receipt data to any third parties. Your financial information stays on your device.

🎯 How We Use Your Information

All data collected by Receipt is used solely to provide app functionality:

  • Display and organize your scanned receipts
  • Extract and store receipt information
  • Enable searching through your receipts
  • Export receipts when you choose to share them

🛡️ Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

All your receipts and app data are stored locally on your device using iOS's secure data storage mechanisms. We do not maintain servers or databases with your information.

No Cloud Sync

Receipt does not offer cloud synchronization. Your data remains exclusively on your device, giving you complete control.

Important: We do not store your receipts or personal data on our servers. Everything stays on your device.

🚫 What We Don't Do

Receipt is committed to respecting your privacy. We explicitly do NOT:

  • ❌ Use advertising networks or display ads
  • ❌ Include analytics or user tracking
  • ❌ Use third-party SDKs for tracking
  • ❌ Collect device identifiers or personal information
  • ❌ Monitor your usage patterns or behavior
  • ❌ Share your data with advertisers or data brokers
  • ❌ Store your data on our servers
  • ❌ Require user registration or accounts
  • ❌ Access your financial accounts

⚖️ Your Rights and Choices

You have complete control over your data in Receipt:

  • Access: You have full access to all your receipt data within the app
  • Export: You can export your receipts at any time
  • Deletion: You can delete individual receipts or clear all data from within the app
  • Camera Control: You can enable or disable camera access in iOS Settings

👶 Children's Privacy

Receipt does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is designed to be safe for users of all ages, with all data stored locally on the device.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. When we make changes:

  • We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Significant changes will be communicated through app updates
  • Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy
