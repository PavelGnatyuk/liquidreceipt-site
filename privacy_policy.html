Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Receipt is built to work offline with on-device processing.

Last updated: December 19, 2025

Privacy at a glance

Clear, simple, and strict.

No ads. No trackers. No analytics.
Receipt stores your receipts and financial information locally on your device — we never see or access your data.

Information we collect

Receipt collects minimal information only when you actively use specific features:

Data type Purpose When collected
Receipt images Store your scanned receipts as PDFs When you scan or import receipts
Extracted data Store merchant, date, total, line items Automatically during scanning (on-device OCR)
Comments Store your notes on receipts When you add typed or dictated comments
Folder structure Organize your receipts When you create and organize folders
Currency settings Track exchange rates and default currency When you configure currency preferences
Important: All processing happens locally on your device using Apple frameworks. Receipt does not send your data to external servers.

Local data storage

Receipt stores all your data locally on your device using Apple’s SwiftData framework. This includes:

  • Scanned receipt PDFs (multi-page supported)
  • Extracted data (merchant names, dates, totals, line items)
  • Folder organization and structure
  • Your comments and notes
  • Currency preferences and exchange rates
  • App settings

Camera usage

Receipt uses your device’s camera to scan receipts:

  • Camera access is only activated when you tap scan
  • Photos are converted to PDFs and stored locally
  • Multi-page scanning is supported for long receipts
  • No images are uploaded to external servers
  • You can revoke camera access any time in iOS Settings

Microphone usage

Receipt uses the microphone for voice dictation of comments:

  • Microphone activates only when you tap the dictation button
  • Speech recognition is processed using Apple’s Speech framework
  • Dictated text is stored locally as comments
  • No audio recordings are stored or transmitted
  • You can revoke microphone access any time in iOS Settings

OCR & data extraction

Receipt automatically extracts data from your receipts:

  • OCR uses Apple’s Vision framework entirely on-device
  • Extracted data includes merchant name, date, total, and line items
  • Currency can be detected from receipt text
  • You can manually edit any extracted data
On-device processing: Your receipt images and extracted data never leave your device.

Share extension

Receipt includes a Share Extension to import receipts from other apps:

  • Accept PDFs and images from Files, Mail, Safari, Photos
  • Imported files are processed and stored locally
  • Data sharing uses iOS App Groups
  • No data is transmitted to external services

Third-party services

Receipt is designed to work completely offline using only Apple frameworks:

  • VisionKit & Vision: Document scanning and OCR (Apple, on-device)
  • PDFKit: PDF creation and viewing (Apple, on-device)
  • SwiftData: Local data storage (Apple, on-device)
  • Speech: Dictation for comments (Apple, on-device)

We do not use any third-party services for data storage, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Apple only: No third-party SDKs, no analytics, no tracking.

Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your personal information or receipt data to any third parties.

The only time your data leaves your device is when you choose to:

  • Export receipts as PDF or CSV files
  • Share exported files via the iOS Share Sheet

How we use your information

All data collected by Receipt is used solely to provide app functionality:

  • Store and display scanned receipts
  • Extract and organize receipt information
  • Enable searching by merchant, amount, or text
  • Track currencies and exchange rates
  • Generate export reports (PDF, CSV) when requested

Data storage and security

Local storage

All your receipts and app data are stored locally on your device using iOS secure storage mechanisms. We do not maintain servers or databases with your information.

No cloud sync

Receipt does not offer cloud synchronization. Your data remains exclusively on your device.

No account required

Receipt does not require registration or login.

Important: We do not store your receipts or personal data on our servers. Everything stays on your device.

What we don’t do

Receipt is committed to respecting your privacy. We explicitly do NOT:

  • Use advertising networks or display ads
  • Include analytics or user tracking
  • Use third-party SDKs for tracking
  • Collect device identifiers or personal information
  • Monitor usage patterns or behavior
  • Share data with advertisers or data brokers
  • Store your data on our servers
  • Require user registration or accounts
  • Access your financial accounts or bank information
  • Send data over the internet

Your rights and choices

You have complete control over your data in Receipt:

  • Access: full access to your receipt data within the app
  • Export: export receipts as PDF or CSV at any time
  • Edit: edit extracted data (merchant, total, date, currency)
  • Deletion: delete individual receipts and folders
  • Camera control: enable/disable camera access in iOS Settings
  • Microphone control: enable/disable microphone access in iOS Settings

Children’s privacy

Receipt does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. When we make changes:

  • We update the “Last updated” date
  • Significant changes will be communicated through app updates
  • Continued use constitutes acceptance of the updated policy
