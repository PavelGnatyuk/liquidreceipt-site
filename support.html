❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan a receipt? Open the app and tap the scan button. Point your camera at the receipt and the app will automatically capture it.
Does Receipt work offline? Yes! Receipt works completely offline. No internet connection is required to scan, view, or manage your receipts.
Where are my receipts stored? All your receipts are stored locally on your device. We never upload your data to any server or cloud service.
Can I export my receipts? Yes! You can export individual receipts or share them via iOS's standard sharing options.
Is my financial data safe? Absolutely. Your receipt data never leaves your device. We don't have access to your receipts or any personal information.
