FAQ

Tap to expand.

How do I scan a receipt? ›
Open the app, select or create a folder, then tap scan. Point your camera at the receipt — multi‑page scanning is supported for long receipts.
What data is extracted automatically? ›
Receipt uses Apple’s Vision framework to extract merchant name, date, total amount, and line items (depending on receipt quality).
Can I edit the extracted data? ›
Yes. You can edit merchant, total, currency, date, and exchange rate. Edited fields are visually marked.
What currencies are supported? ›
Receipt supports 7 currencies: USD, EUR, GBP, RUB, ILS, GEL, and BYN. Currency can be detected from text; you can also add custom currencies with your own exchange rates.
How do I export my receipts? ›
Export a combined PDF (all receipts together) and/or a CSV with extracted fields (merchant, date, total, currency, comments) — great for expense reports.
Can I share receipts from other apps? ›
Yes. Use the iOS Share Extension to send PDFs and images from Files, Mail, Safari, or Photos directly to Receipt.
Does Receipt work offline? ›
Yes. Scanning, organizing, searching, and exporting all work offline. No servers, no accounts.
Where are my receipts stored? ›
Locally on your device as PDFs. We never upload your receipts to any server or cloud service.
Can I add notes to receipts? ›
Yes. Add comments by typing or dictation — perfect for context like “Client dinner” or “Hotel • 3 nights”.
